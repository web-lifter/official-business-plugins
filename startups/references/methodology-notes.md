# Methodology and provenance limits

The router combines customer-development, canvas and Lean Startup workflows. See [sources](../SOURCES.md). Its rules are repository heuristics, not a reproduced curriculum or validated decision model. The repository does not include the curriculum formerly referenced by this file's callers; chapter/row claims must not be treated as verified citations.

Counts such as five interviews, three competitors or fourteen days are prompts to inspect evidence, not sufficient sample-size or market-fit criteria. Prefer representative evidence, falsifiable hypotheses, preregistered metrics and measured outcomes. Incomplete tests need execution or data collection before a learning card. Pivots and refinements are distinct; do not count refinements as pivots.
