# Startups methodology sources

This catalogue records the methods attributed in the existing skill references. It does not bundle the original books, verify page/chapter quotations, or establish that every local decision rule appears in those sources. Consult each skill's `reference.md` or `references.md` for the specific claimed attribution; verify precise citations against the original edition before publishing them.

| Method | Source attributed by the existing materials | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Customer development | Steve Blank, *The Four Steps to the Epiphany*; Blank and Dorf, *The Startup Owner's Manual* | Local stage gates are heuristics, not proof of validation. |
| Business model canvas | Osterwalder and Pigneur, *Business Model Generation* | Canvas completeness is not empirical validation. |
| Value proposition canvas | Osterwalder and colleagues, *Value Proposition Design* | Mapping coverage and market fit are different. |
| Validated learning and MVP | Eric Ries, *The Lean Startup*; Ash Maurya, *Running Lean* | Version/edition-specific claims require checking the source. |
| Business experiments | Bland and Osterwalder, *Testing Business Ideas* | Test design, execution and interpretation remain separate steps. |
| Retention modelling | Constant-period survival model; derived algebra in `skills/churn-model/` | `r` is retention; survival is `r**n`. No industry benchmark is implied. |
